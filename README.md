<h2>📱 How the Digital Business Profile &amp; Dual QR Code System Works</h2>
<p>Whenever a user creates a card on your platform, the <strong>vCard Management System</strong> generates a unique, shareable profile URL along with two ready-to-use QR codes: a <strong>Standard QR Code</strong> and a printable <strong>Desk Standee QR Code</strong>. Business owners can print the Standee QR code and keep it on their office table, reception counter, or billing desk.</p>

<p>When any customer or client scans that QR code using their smartphone camera, your user's complete <strong>digital business profile</strong> opens instantly in their mobile browser—no mobile app installation needed. Visitors can read about the business, check opening hours, view past work in the gallery, save the contact file directly to their phonebook, or fill out the inquiry form to connect with the owner directly on WhatsApp.</p>
